A body assembled from the wreckage of a machine that once served pages. The figure is not an image — it is a signed-distance body, modeled and raymarched live, skinned with the interface behind it.

Move to corrupt the signal. Click for catastrophic failure — the page re-renders, the body scatters and re-forms.

The sound is the same failure engine: ring-buffer stutter, tape-stop varispeed, dropout clicks, sample-rate decimation, dithered bit-collapse. Headphones recommended.

Nothing loads clean anymore. The error is the aesthetic.

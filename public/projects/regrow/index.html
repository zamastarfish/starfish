regrow

A picture book of urban rewilding, drawn and sung entirely by code. Eight pages: a grey city, seeds on the wind, a sprout in a crack, and slowly the green comes back.

Tap the big arrows to turn the pages. Tap anywhere in a scene — every page answers with birds, blooms, ripples, or butterflies.

Nothing was ever lost. It was only waiting for a crack.
